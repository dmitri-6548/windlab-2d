Model fidelity

Real fluid grid, real wake dynamics. A D2Q9 lattice-Boltzmann solver applies no-slip bounce-back at the body. Readouts blend the unsteady pressure signal with a sectional aerodynamic model.
What the numbers mean

This is a real-time, two-dimensional incompressible approximation. It captures separation, recirculation, and vortex shedding for comparison, but finite grid size and the absence of 3D effects mean it is not a substitute for engineering CFD or wind-tunnel validation.

Quick guide

  1. Select New shape, then add straight segments with Line or drag smooth segments with Curve.
  2. Select Close shape. Crossing outlines are rejected because they do not define one solid body.
  3. Select Pivot and click anywhere on the canvas. Rotation in Test mode occurs around that exact point.
  4. Enter Test, adjust air speed and angle, and switch between speed, vorticity, and pressure fields.
Reading the wake: alternating red and blue structures in Vorticity view are counter-rotating vortices. Pressure view shows high pressure in warm colors and suction in cool colors.
